# KPI Framework

## Purpose

This KPI framework defines the primary business metrics used in the Customer & Sales Analytics project.

The goal is to ensure that analytical calculations are connected to measurable business questions and decision-making.

---

## Sales KPIs

| KPI                 | Definition                          | Business Question                  |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue       | Sum of order values                 | How much revenue was generated?    |
| Average Order Value | Average revenue per order           | What is the typical order value?   |
| Order Volume        | Total number of orders              | How many transactions occurred?    |
| Revenue by Customer | Revenue attributed to each customer | Who generates the most revenue?    |
| Revenue by Product  | Revenue attributed to each product  | Which products drive revenue?      |
| Revenue Trend       | Revenue over time                   | How is sales performance changing? |

---

## Customer KPIs

| KPI                           | Definition                                 | Business Question                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Customer Lifetime Spend       | Total recorded spending per customer       | Which customers have the highest value? |
| Average Customer Spend        | Average customer spending                  | What is typical customer value?         |
| Repeat Purchase Rate          | Customers with multiple purchases          | How strong is customer retention?       |
| Customer Revenue Contribution | Customer revenue relative to total revenue | How concentrated is revenue?            |
| Customer Satisfaction         | Average customer rating                    | How satisfied are customers?            |

---

## Product KPIs

| KPI                   | Definition                         | Business Question                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Units Sold            | Total quantity sold                | Which products have the highest demand?    |
| Product Revenue       | Revenue generated by product       | Which products contribute most to revenue? |
| Product Rank          | Relative revenue or sales position | Which products are top performers?         |
| Average Selling Price | Average product selling price      | How does pricing vary?                     |

---

## Inventory KPIs

| KPI                             | Definition                           | Business Question                       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Units in Stock                  | Current recorded inventory           | What inventory is available?            |
| Units Sold                      | Historical quantity sold             | Which products have high demand?        |
| Sales-to-Inventory Relationship | Demand compared with available stock | Which products may face stock-out risk? |

---

## Customer Satisfaction KPIs

| KPI                                     | Definition                               | Business Question                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Average Rating                          | Mean customer rating                     | What is overall satisfaction?                    |
| Low-Rating Rate                         | Share of lower-rated reviews             | Where are customer experience concerns?          |
| Product Rating                          | Average rating by product                | Which products require investigation?            |
| High-Spend / Low-Satisfaction Customers | High-value customers with weaker ratings | Which customers may require retention attention? |

---

## KPI Design Principles

KPIs should be:

* Business-relevant
* Clearly defined
* Reproducible
* Based on validated data
* Interpretable by non-technical stakeholders
* Connected to a potential business action
