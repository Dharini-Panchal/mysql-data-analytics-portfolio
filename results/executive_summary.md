# Executive Summary

## Customer & Sales Analytics — MySQL

### Business Objective

This project analyzes customer, transaction, product, inventory, and customer-review data for a simulated retail business.

The objective is to transform operational data into actionable business insights that can support:

* Revenue growth
* Customer retention
* Product performance management
* Inventory planning
* Customer experience improvement
* Data quality and operational decision-making

---

## Analytical Approach

The analysis follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow:

1. Data profiling
2. Data quality assessment
3. Data cleaning and standardization
4. KPI development
5. Customer analytics
6. Sales and revenue analysis
7. Product performance analysis
8. Inventory risk analysis
9. Customer satisfaction analysis
10. Advanced SQL analysis
11. Business insight generation
12. Recommendations

---

## Key Business Questions

The analysis investigates:

* What are the organization's core sales KPIs?
* Which customers contribute the most revenue?
* Which customers demonstrate repeat purchasing behaviour?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which products show potential inventory risk?
* Which products receive lower customer ratings?
* Which high-value customers may require retention attention?
* How does customer spending change between purchases?
* Where are potential data quality issues?
* Which business areas require further investigation?

The analysis is designed to help business stakeholders identify opportunities related to:

| Business Area       | Analytical Focus                             |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Revenue             | Sales performance and revenue contribution   |
| Customers           | High-value and repeat customers              |
