# Customer Analytics — Findings

## Objective

Understand customer value, purchasing behaviour and potential retention opportunities.

## KPIs

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Customers | 10 |
| Average Customer Spend | 25.37 |
| Highest Customer Spend | 90.42 |
| Customers With Multiple Orders | 5 |

## Key Findings

### 1. High-value customers

The analysis identified the customers contributing the highest levels of revenue.

### 2. Customer segmentation

Customers were segmented into High Value, Medium Value, Low Value and Very Low Value groups based on total spending.

### 3. Inactive customers

Customers with no matching order records were identified using a LEFT JOIN.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention initiatives for high-value customers.
- Investigate inactive customers for potential re-engagement campaigns.
- Monitor changes in customer order values.
- Combine spending and satisfaction metrics to identify at-risk high-value customers.
